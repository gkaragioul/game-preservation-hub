# Condemned 2: Bloodshot — independent modernization research

**Project status:** private, playable PC research build. **Public release:** none. The developer reports playing through the entire campaign with keyboard and mouse. That playthrough has not been independently reproduced.

## What is this project?

*Condemned 2: Bloodshot* was [developed by Monolith Productions](https://warnerbrosgames.com/index.php/article/monolith-productions-celebrates-25-years-of-videogame-history) and [published by SEGA for Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/24247/condemned-2-bloodshottm/). Released in 2008, it is known for visceral melee combat. This is an independent attempt to understand what it would take to preserve and modernize that console experience for a present-day PC. It is **not** an official remake, a retail-game download, or a released port. RLabs is not affiliated with the game's owners and has no grant of rights from them.

The research asks whether a game built for older console hardware can reach a usable PC experience. The developer has played the entire campaign with keyboard and mouse in a private build. That playthrough establishes the project's practical playability, while individual controls and release-readiness milestones still need their own evidence.

## Where does the project stand?

- **Playable campaign:** on 18 September 2026, the developer confirmed playing through the full campaign with keyboard and mouse. The recovery, playable-menu, and first-level milestones are marked complete on that basis. This is a developer playthrough, not a separately reproduced test of the full campaign.
- **Mouse-look:** the PC keyboard-and-mouse mouse-look milestone is accepted for its defined scope, based on the developer's decision and recorded measurements.
- **Controls settings:** the unified input milestone is still in progress. A reviewed private build reached the Controls screen with Keyboard & Mouse selected, but an in-game editing page, visible pointer, rebinding, and persistence after restart have not yet been shown in the required visible test. Controller and hotplug checks also remain open.

The private build is playable; there is no public game executable, source release, gameplay package, or independently reproducible PC playthrough from this project. The project's formal recovery detector has a narrower result than the developer's campaign playthrough, so it should not be read as independent proof of every later milestone.

## What would have to happen next?

The next useful proof is a complete, visible controls flow: open the Keyboard & Mouse editor, use the pointer, change a binding, and confirm the change survives a restart. The remaining input-device tests, stability, performance, and compatibility need separate testing. A public or commercial release would also require a distinct review of the game assets, where the code and assets came from, and the rights held by the appropriate owners. Playability does not grant those rights or make the build ready to distribute.
