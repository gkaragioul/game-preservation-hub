*********************************
INSTALLATION
*********************************
Copy head.bsp into your 'heretic II\base\maps' directory

To run the map, call up the console (~) and type "map head"

*********************************
INFORMATION
*********************************
Single Player:  Yes
Difficulty:     All
Co-op:          No
Death Match:    Four to Twelve players suggested

Map Details:
Although playable as a solo map, with a challenging mix of
monsters and a familiar End Boss, Head is a fun deathmatch
level which features several shrines, three arenas (the
Floating Monolith, the Dripping Cavern, and the Lava God)
connected by halls and teleports. Includes support for
the Heretic II Enhancement Pack!
