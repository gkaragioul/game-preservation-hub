SSAsylum - By Voltais

The Silver Spring Asylum... Bordering the Darkmire Swamps, it was the prime target
of Operation First Strike, the beginning of Morcalavins reign of terror with the plague.
With a force of Plague Ssithra, Plague Spreaders, various beasts of the land and the
Asylums own, now infected, inhabitants, Morcalavin begins his spread of terror. But there's
one young guard, hiding in the kitchen, that he missed...
