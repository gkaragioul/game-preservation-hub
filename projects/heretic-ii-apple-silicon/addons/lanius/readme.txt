- The Story -


Morcalavin has been defeated, and with him the plague
that ravaged Parthoris is no more. 

However there is much in the land to put right. The 
elders of the elves, the ssithra, the k'chekrik, and 
even the ogles all agree that the seven Tomes of Power 
are far too dangerous to be kept in one place. The 
temptation to use them - or misuse them - would be too 
great. 

The seven Tomes were separated, scattered throughout 
Parthoris. Corvus still keeps his Tome. Siernan the 
ssithra healer was entrusted with one, as was the 
k'chekrik high priestess. The ogles, ever the most 
trustworthy, have been entrusted with two of the Tomes, 
hidden deep within their mountain homes. The remaining 
two have been entrusted to the two most powerful of 
elven families, one in Silverspring, the other in Fairbury
far to the south. 

Your name is Lanius, of an ancient and noble Silverspring 
family. Your clan managed to leave Silverspring before
the  insane Celestial Watcher closed off the city, and were 
fortunate enough to avoid contracting the plague. You are 
the keeper of one of the Tomes of Power, a sacred duty you 
do not take lightly. 

Word was come out of the unexplored lands far to the east 
that elves are being taken captive and used as slaves. The
cauthorians - a race of reptilian assassins - are responsible. 
Since they were immune to the plague, their compliance with 
Morcalavin has been condemned by the rest of Parthoris. 

Long ago the cauthorians built great cities, the greatest
of which was the city of Cauthor S'gath. D'sparil's armies 
destroyed the cauthorian empire, and since then they have 
roamed Parthorisas nomads, living mostly in small family-based 
clans. With the rise of Morcalavin, the cauthorians realised 
they had only two choices: either ally themselves with the 
immortal seraph, or face total and final destruction as a 
people. 

Now that Morcalavin's reign is over, the assassins are 
taking advantage of the chaos he has left behind. Using 
elven slaves they are rebuilding their once great empire, 
starting with the old capital city Cauthor S'gath. They 
have been united under a new and ambitious leader, one 
who goes by the name of Shadow Slayer. 

The other peoples of Parthoris fear the assassins will 
not stop at simply regaining their former lands. Your 
mission is to journey to the very heart of the old 
cauthorian homeland, to destroy Shadow Slayer before the 
empire can rise anew. It is hoped that the death of Shadow 
Slayer will cause the cauthorian people to fragment back 
into smaller nomadic clans, which are of little threat to 
anyone. 

But be warned: Shadow Slayer is a powerful magician and 
accomplished warrior. And just getting to Cauthor S'Gath 
will be a trial in itself.

- System Requirements -


You will need: 

Heretic II installed (the full version) 
The Expansion Pack version 1.06 (not essential, but recommended) 
The Shadow Slayer files installed in the correct directory 
Around 14 megabytes of disk space 
The basic system requirements to run Heretic II 
(Refer to the help files included with Hr2)

Without the expansion pack you won't have the whirlwind 
defensive spell, and some of the on-screen text won't appear 
correctly. Version 1.0 of Heretic II also won't let you see 
the Lanius skin. The game should still run though, just not 
at its best. 

Each level has been designed to run well under software 
video mode, so even a low-end video card should be sufficient 
to run the game. 
 
======================================================

- Installation -


Everything you need is included in one ZIP file. Simply 
unzip the file directly into your Heretic II directory on 
your harddrive (usually C:\Program Files/Heretic II/), 
and all the correct files and directories will be created. 
The ZIP file will create a new directory called Lanius. 
To run the game, simply double-click on the play_sslayer.bat 
file under Heretic II/Lanius/. Or use the shortcut called 
play_sslayer.lnk, which you can copy to your desktop (if you 
have Heretic II installed in C:\Program Files\). Starting 
the game any other way will not display the correct in-game 
messages, and some custom sounds won't work. 

By default the game starts with an 800x600 screen in software 
video mode. A crosshair for aiming spells and weapons will 
appear (with auto-aiming off) and the blood level will be 
set to ultraviolent. You can change any of these defaults 
using the menu system, and your own settings will always 
be loaded from then on.

- Credits -


Obivously the makers and distributors of Heretic II 
(Raven Software and Activision respectively) did most 
of the original work. 

The silver and gold breastplate models were extracted from 
the original Corvus, with a few alterations (internal faces 
were added, the skins were modified). The elf head on a 
stake, and the hanged elf, are modified from the plague-elf 
model. Most of the skins are modifications of the original 
Heretic II versions. 

The fire imp model was created by the makers of Hexen II 
(another Raven/Activision product), with a few modifications 
(resizing the skin). 

The skeleton model was derived mostly from a Hexen II skull, 
with the ribs and backbone added. The skin has been slightly 
altered from the original (resized, colour changed). 

The grate texture used in the arena level has been 
modified from one that appeared in the original Heretic 
(Raven Games / ID Software). 

Levels and skins were created using QuArK (Quake Army Knife).
