Synopsis to the included levels island1 and island2
**********************************************************

map name-	island1
full map name-	A Forgotten Island
type of play-	Single Player
about-		This level is the first part of what was going to be several parts of an island.  It has a Tomb Raider-like atmosphere and plays like it too; you have to figure out what to do.  Nice waterfalls, jungle scene.

map name-	island2
full map name-	Waterfall
type of play-	Single Player
about-		The second part.  Huge waterfall (and yes, the water moves, unlike a lot of maps I've seen)  Harder than the first one.



What to do:
-copy files island1 and island2 and put them in ...Heretic II\base\maps\
-open Heretic II and at console type "map island1"
