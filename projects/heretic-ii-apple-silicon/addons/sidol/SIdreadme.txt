IMPORTANT NOTE: This SP addon can only be played with Heretic 2 v1.06 .

I. Story:
 The Sacred Idol has been stolen! You must find and take it back. However the
wizard who stole the Idol won't let it go easily and sent many monsters to stop
you from taking it back. Some of the sent monsters are fire imps.
 You'll start in Imp Town to look for a way to reach Vardia where the wizard
hides the Idol. Rumor says there are 2 ways to reach Vardia from Imp Town. Any 
chosen path doesn't matter as long you can find the Idol. Beware that you have to
face the wizard in the end and it won't be an easy fight.

II. Instructions:

1. Unzip all files to your heretic2 folder. Each files have different paths but
don't worry about them cause the paths will be created in unzipping process.
2. Copy your user.cfg from /user (under your heretic2 folder) to /Suser to use
your configuration in this SP addon.
3. Start this SP addon by running SIdol.bat.

III. Information:
 These SP addon is a patched version from earlier version which was released in
3 zips. The updates are:
1. Rivers with currents use better texture.
2. Path to woods in Imp Town 2 is visible now.
3. Difficulty modified.
4. Text messages are available now.
5. Sewer map modified to support coop.
6. Many map improvements (adding beds, triggers, windows etc).

 These SP addon support coop mode but doesn't support DM mode.
 This addon also features Imps. It only requires one skin but I include 2 other
skins. Its skins and model are located in SIdol/models/monsters/imp. You can
choose the skin by renaming the current skin (!skin.m8) to other name and rename
other skin (imp0.m8 or imp1.m8) to !skin.m8 . 

IV. There are some notes about these addon:
1. I don't force you to take any weapons and spells available in the maps but the
conscequence is if you don't take a weapon (e.g hellstaff) in a map, you MAY not
find it anymore in other maps.
2. Most of the maps do not allow returning to previous maps, however the last 4
maps (Vardia maps) allow you to do so. So once you reached Vardia, you can go to
other maps and come back later.
3. Most maps have multiple paths and some of them have branches or multiple
exits which lead to different map so if you are bored going through the same
paths or maps, you can try other paths.
4. Most maps have secrets. Knowledge and skill are required to get them. They are
optional of course, but useful weapons or spells might be there.
5. You can only carry 4 key items and only one kind can be carried at a time.
6. Some maps contains bosses in the form of heads which shoots projectiles from
their mouth. Shoot inside their mouth to defeat the monsters hiding inside.
7. In Imp Town and Vardia, goodies such as manas, ammos and potions are placed
inside houses and building so you have to find buildings with unlocked doors to
get the goodies. (Hint: their doors use different texture than locked doors)
8. Most maps are big (long at least) so if you can't see far enter this in the
console:
 r_farclipdist 8192
 
 The default is 4096 so this should double the view range. You can set more than
8192 if you like.

V. Hints and tips:
1. Try to get more available key items than required. See if any of them can be
used elsewhere. You might get something good from it ;).
2. Pray to some statues to get their blessings ;). Some statues allow you to pray
more than once.
3. Imp's fireballs are deadly so do your best to dodge them.
4. Although imps like to dodge your shots, there is a spell that imps won't
dodge. Find it to aid you because you will meet imps in all maps!!
5. Watch out for traps!
6. You can stand on large bushes and on trees.
7. Set autoaim on if you have problem aiming when fighting bosses in form of a
head.
8. Some enemies don't have high shooting elevation angle. If you know who they 
are, use that weakness for your advantage.
9. In Vardia, the Sacred Idol is kept in Lake Temple but there're many things to
do before you can get the Idol.
