Single Player Heretic II Map

Title                   : Andoria Waterworks
Release Date		: 2020-09-12
Author                  : whirledtsar
Email Address           : whirls98@gmail.com
Other Files By Author   : Shifting Sands and Tyrant's Tome for Hexen 2; Wolf Chapel Revisit and Lepistus for Hexen; a map in HUMP Mini for Heretic; maps in XUMP and Hexmas for Hexen; "Medevil" for Doom 2; a map in Mayhem 19 for Doom 2
Description             : A new singleplayer map for Heretic II set in Andoria. Due to engine & compiler limitations, it unfortunately had to be split into 2 map files. It's a medium-sized level (~25 minutes) with a focus on action as well as some traps and rope-swinging. Many but not all of the weapons & defensive weapons appear - even the Enhancement Patch's Whirlwind spell. Tested on the Action difficulty. There is no story, so if you wish you can imagine that it takes place at some point during Corvus' original journeys through Andoria. He even has the sickly plague skin for it. There are three unmarked secrets to find, as well as a few intentional sequence breaks/shortcuts you can accomplish with acrobatics.
Making that animated waterfall texture work was a fucking insane pain in the dick that nearly made me tear my hair out, so you better appreciate it.
To play, copy contents to your "Heretic 2/base" folder and use the included batch file.
Additional Credits to   : Raven Software; Trenchbroom devs for Ht2 support
