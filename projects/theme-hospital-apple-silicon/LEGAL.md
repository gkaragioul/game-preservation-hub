# Rights and Distribution Boundary

This repository is source-only compatibility and preservation work.

## Included

- CorsixTH source and open project resources.
- Apple Silicon and Metal renderer changes.
- Build files and documentation.

## Not Included

- Theme Hospital game files or extracted data.
- GOG or other commercial installers.
- Original graphics, audio, movies, manuals, or avatars.
- User saves, configuration, screenshots, or private paths.
- Generated playable app bundles containing commercial data.

Users must provide their own legally obtained Theme Hospital files. All rights to Theme Hospital and its original content belong to their respective owners.

Public binary releases require a fresh audit of bundled files and third-party library licenses, plus Apple Developer ID signing and notarization.
