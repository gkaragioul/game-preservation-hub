Name of Map -			cauthor
Full Name -			Cauthor S'Gath
Created By -			Dann Pigdon (2003)

Single player -			As the 4th level of the Shadow Slayer add-on
Coop -                          (As above)
Deathmatch -			Five spawn points
Special Features -		Uses a separate armour model
				(/lanius/models/objects/armour/)

Description -			This is the only map included with the Shadow Slayer
				add-on that also functions as a deathmatch map.
				After defeating Shadow Slayer, invite some 'friends'
				to explore the abandoned city - and then kill them!
				Weapons include the hellstaff, solar flare, firewall,
                                the mana bow & the phoenix bow (look underground). 
				Defensive spells include lightning shield, doom cloud, 
				morph ovum, and whirlwind. Shrines include mana (look
				for the sparkles), blade and armour (as an armour model).
